Sample Jira-style bug report

Sample
SHOP-142 Bug Open

Coupon discount stays applied after the qualifying item is removed from the cart

Priority
High
Component
Cart
Platform
Web
Version
2.4.1
Environment
UAT, Chrome on Windows 11
Reporter
Christian Jay Javal

Steps to reproduce

  1. Add the Trail Backpack to the cart.
  2. Apply the coupon SAVE20, which is valid for backpacks only.
  3. Confirm the cart total drops by 20%.
  4. Remove the backpack from the cart.
  5. Add a Water Bottle to the cart.

Expected result

The coupon is removed, or rejected, because the cart no longer has a qualifying item. The Water Bottle is shown at full price.

Actual result

SAVE20 stays applied and takes 20% off the Water Bottle.

Impact

Customers can get discounts on items the coupon is not valid for.
